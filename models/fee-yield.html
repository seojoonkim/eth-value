Formula

(Burned L1 Fees + Burned Blob Fees) × 365 ÷ APR ÷ Supply

How it works

Reverse-engineers fair value from live staking APR, treating ETH like a yield-bearing bond. If Ethereum generates X in annual fees and the current staking yield is Y%, the implied market cap is X ÷ Y%. Uses real-time Lido stETH APR instead of a fixed target yield. This approach is favored by TradFi analysts evaluating crypto as an alternative asset class. Burned fees fall on weekends (last 6 months: weekend median about half of weekdays, matching on-chain base-fee burn), so the daily line dips every Sat/Sun. This is real activity, not missing data.

Limits: Assumes stable yield environment; actual staking yields are volatile in practice
